# Prompts

Every image was made in ChatGPT (Warren's Plus account, chatgpt.com in Chrome).
Each prompt starts with the same art-direction header so the set matches.

## Art direction header (top of every prompt)

> DRIFT ART DIRECTION. Editorial gouache-and-ink illustration with layered
> cut-paper depth, like a natural-history plate printed in a limited palette.
> Palette only: petrol teal #0E2F36, deep sea #1F5A5E, sea glass #7FB7AE,
> foam #E6EEE9; man-made plastic is the only thing allowed in dye magenta
> #FF4D8D, oxidised amber #F2B544 and bleached bone #F1E9D2. Light: soft
> diffuse daylight from above, long god-rays under water, no harsh specular
> highlights. Rendering: matte gouache, fine visible paper grain, crisp
> silhouettes, no outlines, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.

Asset prompts add: *"Transparent background PNG. One subject only, centred,
nothing else in the frame. Largest size available."*
