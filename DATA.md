# Data

Every dataset used by Drift, where it came from, its licence, when it was
fetched, and how it was processed. `public/data/credits.json` is generated
from this table by `scripts/data/make_credits.py` (same rows, cells verbatim). Fetch and processing scripts live in
`scripts/data/`; raw downloads are cached in `scripts/data/cache/` (not committed).

| Dataset | Source | Licence | Fetched | Processing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OSCAR surface currents (1/3 deg, 5-day, 2011-12-06..2014-09-26) | https://coastwatch.pfeg.noaa.gov/erddap/griddap/jplOscar (NASA/JPL PO.DAAC OSCAR via NOAA CoastWatch ERDDAP; Bonjean and Lagerloef 2002) | Free to use and redistribute, no warranty (ERDDAP dataset licence) | 2026-10-13 | Every 3rd grid point (1 deg), averaged by calendar month ignoring NaN, shifted to 1 deg cell centres by a NaN-aware 4-point mean, lon wrapped to -180..180, packed as int16 at 0.001 m/s in 12 DRFT tiles (`public/data/currents/clim-01..12.bin`) |
| QuikSCAT SeaWinds monthly 10 m winds (RSS science quality, 1999-08..2009-10) | https://coastwatch.pfeg.noaa.gov/erddap/griddap/erdQSwindmday (Remote Sensing Systems, http://www.remss.com/) | Free to use and redistribute, no warranty (ERDDAP dataset licence) | 2026-10-13 | Every 16th point (2 deg, 75S..75N), averaged by calendar month, monthly-mean speeds over 20 m/s masked (sea-ice contamination), then every remaining no-data cell (coast, sea ice, land; 36% of cells) filled per month by iterative nearest-valid-neighbour extrapolation (repeated NaN-aware 3x3 mean, lon wrapping) so the tile has no gaps, packed as int16 at 0.01 m/s in one 12-frame DRFT tile (`public/data/wind/clim.bin`). Replaces the planned ICOADS climatology, which the ERDDAP server stopped serving |
| NOAA Global Drifter Program 6-hourly QC drifters, 2012-01-01..2014-09-26 | https://erddap.aoml.noaa.gov/gdp/erddap/tabledap/drifter_6hour_qc (Lumpkin and Centurioni 2019, doi:10.25921/7ntx-z961) | CC BY 4.0 | 2026-10-13 | One fix per drifter-day (`orderByClosest("ID,time/1day")`), consecutive-day pairs with displacement under 300 km, windage = fix on/after drogue loss, joined to the sampled monthly current and wind fields (878,086 pairs from 4,079 drifters, 2012-01-01..2014-09-25; 9,747 pairs without a current or wind sample dropped: OSCAR coast gaps and poleward of the 75 deg wind grid); training pairs only (not shipped) |
| Natural Earth 1:50m land | https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector (geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson) | Public domain | 2026-10-13 | Polygons rasterised to a 2048x1024 equirectangular PNG, 255 = land (`public/data/landmask.png`) |
